Who We Are

A High-Capacity Industrial Partner for Nigeria's Most Demanding Marine & Energy Projects

CP Ice Marine Limited is an integrated offshore logistics, marine infrastructure, and industrial contracting firm designed to deliver heavy-duty operational support for large-scale energy, maritime, and civil engineering projects.

Positioned as a high-capacity industry operator, we combine advanced engineering assets, rugged logistics fleets, and robust technical management to handle large-scale contracts, driving industrial growth, infrastructure development, and offshore efficiency across Nigeria.

We operate as a major industrial partner to multinational energy producers, port developers, and civil infrastructure clients, built to withstand the most demanding marine and land environments Nigeria's energy sector presents.
